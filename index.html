How it reads

This is the voice of the whole book.

No hype, no hedging, no padding. It explains the thing, tells you how sure we actually are, and then tells you what to do about it. Two hundred pages of this.

Almost nothing about you is constant. Your body temperature swings by most of a degree across a day. Your alertness, your appetite, your blood pressure, your reaction time and the hormones behind all of them rise and fall on a cycle that repeats roughly every twenty-four hours.

You do not feel this as a rhythm. You feel it as a mood, or as the day being difficult, or as needing another coffee. It is neither. It is a clock, and it is set almost entirely by light.
